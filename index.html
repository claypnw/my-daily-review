Ro launches healthcare research unit, plans to offer data to drugmakers▶ Listen

Reuters — November 6, 2026

  • Telehealth firm Ro announced on Friday it is launching a healthcare research lab powered by artificial intelligence to study patient outcomes and potentially offer insights to drugmakers.
  • Chief Executive Zach Reitano said increased data could provide insights for insurers weighing coverage of expensive medications, allow drugmakers to identify which patients benefit from a specific treatment, and improve health outcomes for individuals on its platform.

India's Coforge joins SpaceX partner network to deploy Grok AI tools for enterprises▶ Listen

Reuters — November 6, 2026

  • Indian IT services firm Coforge has joined the SpaceX Super Intelligence Partner Network to help companies integrate the Grok artificial intelligence portfolio into their operations, it said on Friday.

Texas is done with data centers until further notice, new governor says▶ Listen

USA Today — November 5, 2026

  • Texas is done with data centers unless the companies behind them prove they will benefit Texans, the Lone Star state's newly elected Democratic governor said Thursday, Nov. 5.
  • Gov.-elect Gina Hinojosa told CNN that data centers have moved into mostly rural communities with no rules and laws to protect Texans, and that the state needs to stop what it is doing with data centers until those protections are in place.
  • Over 80 data centers are operating in the state, the second most in the country, according to data center tracking group Cleanview; another 50 are under construction and more than 120 were planned.

Fox News Voter Poll: 2026 midterm elections usher in a blue wave as Democrats win control of Congress▶ Listen

Fox News — November 4, 2026

  • The Fox News Voter Poll surveyed more than 100,000 registered voters nationwide from Oct. 19 to Nov. 3, 2026, combining online and telephone interviews with in-person surveys of Election Day voters.
  • On AI, voters approached this new political issue with wariness: a majority said it would do more to hurt society, a consensus reached by Democrats, Republicans, and independents alike, while a quarter said it would do more to help.
  • Three-quarters of voters opposed a new data center in their community; those who opposed one backed the Democratic House candidates by 28 points, while those in favor backed Republicans by 45 points.

Similar solar farm plans to boost Berkshire councils' coffers▶ Listen

BBC News — November 4, 2026

  • Two neighbouring Berkshire councils are aiming to generate green energy and money to fund local services by building and running their own large solar farms, within a few miles of each other.
  • Work begins next week to install 43,000 solar panels on fields that Wokingham Borough Council owns in the village of Barkham; Wokingham's solar farm has been costed at 24 million pounds and is expected to turn a profit of more than 1 million pounds annually across a 40-year lifespan.
  • West Berkshire's solar farm will cost 18.6 million pounds to build, and is projected to make 10 million pounds profit over its 40-year lifespan; the schemes are not connected and are expected to be operational by the end of 2027.
