Bulgaria emerges as a surprise leader in Europe's quest for battery storage

Reuters — October 23, 2026

  • Helped by €700 million in European Union grants, Bulgaria has doubled its battery capacity over the last year — to around 6 gigawatts, up from 3 gigawatts — allowing it to store solar energy produced in the day and release it during peak hours in the evening, earning the nickname the "Battery of the Balkans."
  • Bulgaria increased its power exports by 62% this year, to 3.5 terawatt-hours, supplying neighbouring countries when heatwaves in July and August left national grids struggling; it has exported as much as 50,000 megawatt-hours of electricity per day.
  • Greece, by contrast, is lagging in the battery race despite its sunshine: its total storage is expected to reach only one gigawatt by the end of the year, even though its solar capacity more than tripled between 2020 and 2025.
