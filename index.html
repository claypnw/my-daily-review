OpenAI Drug Discovery Swarm Decoded 7.7 Million Brain Cells in 32 Minutes

TechTimes — October 22, 2026

  • OpenAI, PerturbAI, and Scripps Research ran nearly 2,000 GPT-Rosalind agents over a CRISPR atlas of 7.7 million mouse brain cells, one agent per gene disruption, finishing in 32 minutes work estimated at months to a year by hand.
  • The agents built a public "reasoning atlas" and surfaced a lead linking the gene DCTN1 to cholesterol production in neurons — a possible angle on Perry syndrome and other neurodegenerative diseases.
  • The leads are hypotheses, not proven results: the preprint is not yet peer-reviewed and the findings still need laboratory testing.

PHP vulnerabilities explained: Risks, severity and security fixes

The Hindu BusinessLine — October 22, 2026

Digitech Info — October 23, 2026 (announcement October 22, 2026)

Barclays to slash 5,000 jobs in AI-powered cost-cutting drive

Reuters (via HOT 96) — October 22, 2026

South Korean economy's growth likely picked up in Q3 on chip exports: Reuters poll

Reuters (via 94.7 The Beast) — October 23, 2026

OpenAI urges Newsom to strengthen California's AI safety law with additional safeguards

StateScoop — October 22, 2026

  • OpenAI wrote to California Governor Gavin Newsom on October 20 urging stronger amendments to SB 53, the state's frontier AI transparency law.
  • It recommends monitoring models for dangerous behavior during training, better cybersecurity, notice to parties hit by covered cyber incidents, effective shutdown controls, and independent audits.

Vikram Solar to roll out first cells from 9 GW Tamil Nadu facility in Dec 2026

The Hindu BusinessLine (PTI) — October 23, 2026

A coal-to-solar project in Ohio

  • Solar farms in Ohio occupy less land than golf courses, and solar can coexist with some farming.

Helcro Solar: New Rules Cut Commercial Solar Costs by 20% for Businesses, Strata and Farms

Medianet News Hub (Helcro Solar) — October 23, 2026

  • Australia's Small-scale Renewable Energy Scheme expanded on October 1 to cover systems up to 1 megawatt, opening its incentives to commercial buildings, strata properties, farms, and community organisations with systems between 100 kilowatts and 1 megawatt.
  • The expansion cuts the upfront cost of a qualifying mid-scale system by about 20% through the certificate mechanism; a 250 kilowatt system can also save about $50,000 a year on electricity bills.
  • Australian commercial and farm rooftops hold an estimated 80 gigawatts of solar potential, with only 5.6 gigawatts installed so far.
