Arizona approved 107 data center tax breaks before moratorium ▶ Listen

The Arizona Republic — November 8, 2026

  • A pause on Arizona's data center tax incentive was supposed to save the state money, but a rush beat the deadline: of 113 applications submitted between June 15 and June 30, 107 were approved — more than in the life of the program — with three withdrawn and three denied.

Energy security push could lower emissions, Wood Mackenzie says ▶ Listen

Reuters — November 10, 2026

  • Countries prioritising energy security over climate goals could produce lower emissions as they accelerate electrification, consultancy Wood Mackenzie says in its latest Energy Transition Outlook, which models warming from 1.5 to 3 degrees Celsius with a base case of 2.6 degrees by 2100.
  • Solar, wind and battery capacity are set to surge 60% by 2030 and sixfold by 2060 in the base case, while global power demand is on track to double by 2060, driven by electrification, industrial growth and data centre expansion.
  • The transition represents a cumulative investment opportunity of $147 trillion to $189 trillion to 2060; if countries instead prioritise expansion to meet electricity and AI demand, warming could rise above 3 degrees.
