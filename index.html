AI helps Stanford scientists discover a “natural Ozempic” with a surprising advantage▶ Listen

ScienceDaily — November 4, 2026

  • Stanford Medicine researchers used AI, an algorithm called Peptide Predictor scanning roughly 20,000 human protein-coding genes, to identify a naturally occurring molecule, BRP, that suppresses appetite and promotes fat loss in animals.
  • BRP acts selectively in the hypothalamus, unlike semaglutide, and in mice and minipigs cut food intake by up to 50 percent in the following hour; obese mice lost about 3 grams, almost all fat, over 14 days.
  • It is early-stage animal work only — human safety and effectiveness are untested, and a co-founded company aims to advance it toward clinical trials.

AI debt binge isn't crowding out Treasuries, TBAC says▶ Listen

Reuters — November 4, 2026

  • The Treasury Borrowing Advisory Committee said record corporate borrowing to fund AI investment shows no evidence of crowding out demand for long-end US Treasuries, despite last month's bond selloff sending longer yields to their highest since 2002.
  • TBAC said higher yields mainly reflect growth, policy expectations and energy prices, and that any rate effect from tech investment runs through stronger growth, though heavy tech issuance has widened spreads in the sector.
  • It also said foreign demand to finance US tech has added to dollar strength, and recommended easing bank balance-sheet constraints to boost Treasury demand further.

A 30 Day Carnivore Meal Plan You Can Print and Shop From▶ Listen

Carnivore Weekly — November 3, 2026

  • Thirty days, four weeks with one theme per week, four short grocery lists and portions in ounces, built for one or two people over 50 who want to lose weight and cook little: two meals a day is typical, each about 5 to 6 ounces cooked meat for about 30 to 40 grams protein, dropping meat to 3 ounces when eggs are on the plate.
  • Week 1 is ground beef and eggs, Week 2 adds pork and chicken thighs, Week 3 adds steak, fish and bone broth, Week 4 mixes and repeats favourites; do not count calories for the first eight weeks, and most people land around 3 to 5 grams sodium a day.
  • How to tell it is working: hunger between meals quiets by week 2 to 3 and the waist tape moves; expect 3 to 7 pounds off in week one, mostly water, then half to one pound a week over 50, and do not judge the plan before day 60.

Carnivore in Perimenopause: Why Your Cycle Goes Haywire▶ Listen

Carnivore Weekly — November 4, 2026

  • Women who start carnivore at 44 to 52 were often already shifting: in the first three months the chaos is usually both diet and perimenopause, the diet part shows up fast and settles by day 60 to 90, the perimenopause part drifts over years.
  • Diet levers, falling insulin, fast fat loss releasing stored estrogen, broken sleep, stress and under-eating, can delay ovulation, producing a period a week or two late or skipped, a heavier bleed, spotting, or a cycle stretching to 40 to 60 days then shortening again.
  • Sorting rule given: a change appearing within six weeks of changing food and softening by month three is more likely diet; a change creeping for a year or two with hot flashes or night sweats is more likely age, with a see-a-doctor list including bleeding after 12 months without a period, very heavy bleeding, and severe pelvic pain.
