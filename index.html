1. New awards show celebrates AI music and its creators ▶ Listen

Reuters — November 14, 2026

  • The first AI Music Awards ceremony in the United States, held Friday in downtown Los Angeles, celebrated people who created songs at least partly with AI, a field that has sparked criticism and lawsuits while gaining millions of streams and landing on music charts.

6. AI rockets to top of worries about financial system in latest Fed survey ▶ Listen

Reuters — November 13, 2026

  • Risks tied to the artificial intelligence sector surged to become the top-cited concern for financial stability in the Federal Reserve's semi-annual Financial Stability Report, released Friday; 87% of contacts surveyed cited AI as a salient risk, up from 50% in the May survey.
  • Respondents pointed to cyberattacks, complex AI-related financing, and the possibility of an AI earnings slowdown sparking a broad market correction; the second most-cited concern, a broad correction in risk assets, was named by 52%.
  • The Fed found advanced computing-related debt issuance had surged to $680 billion by October 2026, a fivefold increase on all of 2023, and that debt, guarantees and not-yet-commenced leases on this infrastructure could exceed $3 trillion, creating vulnerabilities that need close monitoring.

7. Wholesale inflation rose in October to highest annual rate in nearly four years ▶ Listen

CNN — November 13, 2026

  • Wholesale inflation accelerated in October to its highest annual rate in nearly four years, Bureau of Labor Statistics data showed Friday: the Producer Price Index rose 0.3% in the month and 6.5% over the 12 months ended in October, the highest since November 2022.
  • Massive investment in artificial intelligence technology and equipment is contributing another layer of inflationary pressure for businesses and consumers, CNN reported; electronic components and accessories rose 4.2% in the month, up 31.7% from a year before, the highest annual rate on record.
  • Economist Matt Bush of Guggenheim Investments told CNN those costs show up indirectly in computer, television and software prices, and Friday's PPI data, with the hotter October CPI, are expected to bring a Federal Reserve rate hike when policymakers meet in early December.

12. Some ketogenic diets found to cause mitochondrial stress and lead to inflammation ▶ Listen

Medical Xpress — November 13, 2026

  • New University of Cincinnati Cancer Center research found that a ketogenic diet high in long-chain saturated fatty acids can increase inflammation; the study, led by Cancer Center Director David Gius, was published November 13 in the journal Science Advances.
  • The team gave ketogenic diets made of different fatty acids to mouse models and individual fatty acids to tissue culture models, and found long-chain saturated fatty acids favor an inflammatory immune response, while unsaturated and medium-chain fatty acids tend to favor anti-inflammatory characteristics.
  • The increase in inflammation is mediated through mitochondrial stress signaling and activation of immune cells, and the findings suggest the source of fat matters for the outcome of a ketogenic diet; a clinical trial is underway at UT Health San Antonio and will open soon at the Cancer Center.
