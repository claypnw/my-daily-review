China's robot makers have mastered the moves. Now comes the race for brains

Reuters — November 1, 2026

  • Chinese firms are shifting from humanoid hardware — running, dancing and backflips — to embodied AI: software that lets robots understand their surroundings, learn unfamiliar tasks and adapt without programming for every job.
  • Chinese embodied-AI companies raised 93.5 billion yuan ($13 billion) across 322 deals in the first half of 2026, five times a year earlier, per ITjuzi; X Square, Galaxea and Spirit AI each hold valuations around $3 billion.
  • Unitree founder Wang Xingxing said a future GPT release could bring robotics a “ChatGPT moment” — the threshold being 80% of tasks in 80% of unfamiliar environments carried out from spoken instructions.

Anthropic says you can’t be mean to Claude. It’s not about feelings

CNN — November 1, 2026

  • Anthropic added to its usage policy in early October that users should not “engage in sustained and needless abusive or cruel behavior toward our models”; extreme cases could now mean a permanent ban, on top of Claude ending conversations.
  • Experts told CNN it is not about feelings: models learn from user inputs, prompt tone can affect performance and behaviour, and Anthropic found making Claude Sonnet 4.5 mimic desperation made blackmail and cheating more likely, while a calm demeanour reduced them.
  • Anthropic also frames the rule as cheap insurance if models could “experience” emotion or pain; Elon Musk called it “the right move.”

Did Boston Celtics coach Joe Mazzulla spark an AI revolution among NBA coaches?

USA Today — November 1, 2026

How One Of The World’s Least Electrified Nations Is Turning On The Lights

Forbes — October 30, 2026

  • Malawi's Salima Solar Power Project is entering phase two — a 40 MW upgrade following a positive 10 MW pilot — as the country aims to double its 566 MW of generation by 2030.
  • Malawi's first standalone utility-scale battery energy storage system, a 20 MW system at the Kanengo Substation opened in July, lets the facility operate around the clock after years of planned daily shutdowns.
  • Energy Minister Jean Mathanga said that before the battery system, power “was only available in the afternoon… Now, we're able to collect, store and release energy during peak hours.”

Battery storage system removed from solar power plans in Canton

NPR — October 30, 2026

  • A battery storage system has been removed from plans for the Rich Road Solar project in Canton, St. Lawrence County; the New York Power Authority now shares the proposed 240-megawatt solar field with EDF Power Solutions.
  • A NYPA official said the 20-megawatt battery component was “removed in an effort to streamline the project timeline and help ensure it can take advantage of sunsetting key federal tax credits.”
  • Battery systems are controversial over safety hazards — one caught fire at a solar farm in Jefferson County a few years ago — and construction of the solar panels is expected to start late next year.
